Instant inquiry replies

The demo above, built in Zapier

  • Google FormsNew response
  • Google SheetsAdd row to the lead log
  • AI by ZapierClassify the inquiry
  • AI by ZapierDraft a reply
  • GmailCreate draft
  • SlackSend alert to the owner
The problem
Inquiries arrive all day while every stylist is with a client. Replies wait until a break, and the customer books with whoever answers first.
What I built
Each form response is logged, sorted by type and urgency, and answered with a draft in the salon's own voice. The owner approves each message in Gmail with one click. Complaints and high-value leads trigger an immediate Slack alert.
What it saves
A reply is ready in under a minute, compared with about 20 minutes of typing spread across the day (estimate).

Reminders that keep the chair full

Bookings in, no-shows down

  • Google CalendarEvent starts in 24 hours
  • AI by ZapierWrite a personal reminder
  • GmailSend the reminder
  • Delay by ZapierWait until 2 hours after the visit
  • GmailSend thank-you with survey link
The problem
A forgotten appointment leaves an empty chair and lost income, and nobody has time to send reminders by hand.
What I built
Every booking on the salon calendar gets a friendly reminder the day before, using the customer's name, service and stylist. After the visit, a thank-you email invites a one-minute survey.
How I'd measure it
Compare the no-show rate over four weeks before and four weeks after switching the reminders on.

Supplier invoices logged for you

Month-end bookkeeping without the inbox dig

  • GmailNew email with attachment
  • AI by ZapierRead vendor, date and total
  • Google SheetsAdd row to the expense log
  • Filter by ZapierContinue only if total is over the limit
  • SlackFlag the invoice for review
The problem
Invoices for color, tools and towels pile up in an inbox. Someone spends hours at month-end finding them and typing the numbers into a sheet.
What I built
Invoices sent to a dedicated email label are read by AI and logged in an expense sheet. Anything above an owner-set amount is flagged so unusual costs get noticed the same day.
What it saves
Roughly five minutes of typing per invoice, plus fewer missed or duplicated entries (estimate).

A feedback monitor that scores every response

Quality evaluation for a small business

  • Google FormsNew survey response
  • AI by ZapierScore sentiment and tag the issue
  • Google SheetsAdd row to the feedback log
  • SlackInstant alert on negative feedback
  • Schedule by ZapierEvery Monday morning
  • AI by ZapierSummarize the week
  • GmailEmail the summary to the owner
The problem
Owners read reviews one at a time, so patterns stay hidden and a bad experience can sit unanswered for days.
What I built
Survey answers from the thank-you email are scored and tagged by issue: wait time, staff, price or cleanliness. Negative feedback reaches the owner within minutes with a suggested reply, and a weekly summary shows what keeps coming up. My background is call-center quality evaluation, so I designed the scoring the way I would review a customer interaction: accurate, fair and easy to act on.
What it saves
The owner spots problems the same day and gets a weekly view instead of reading every response (estimate: about 30 minutes a week).
